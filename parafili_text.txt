Many individuals avoid seeking help because of: shame,  stigma,  fear of punishment,  social exclusion,  fear of being misunderstood,  lack of anonymous support options.  As a result, risk may remain hidden until it becomes more severe. Alt küçük cümle: The core problem is not only the presence of risky impulses, but the absence of a safe path to early help. İnsanlar utandıkları için yardım istemiyor; bu da erken müdahaleyi zorlaştırıyor.”  Paraphilia An atypical or uncommon sexual interest. It is not automatically considered a mental disorder. Paraphilic Disorder A paraphilic interest becomes clinically significant when it causes distress, functional impairment, or involves non-consenting individuals, coercion, or potential harm. Sexual Impulse Control Difficulty Difficulty in managing sexual urges, fantasies, or behaviors despite possible psychological, social, ethical, or legal consequences. Consent and Harm The main ethical boundary is not whether a sexual interest is uncommon, but whether it involves consent, safety, age, power imbalance, or risk of harm. Core Problem Many individuals hide these struggles because of shame, stigma, and fear of punishment. This prevents early help-seeking and may increase risk over time. Slide 3 — Why This Problem Matters: Hidden Risk, Harm, and Functioning Why This Problem Matters Paraphilic disorders and sexual impulse control difficulties matter because they can affect both  the individual’s life functioning  and  the safety of others . Harm to the Individual Individuals may experience: intense shame and guilt,  anxiety and fear of disclosure,  social isolation,  impaired relationships,  difficulty controlling impulses,  increased psychological distress,  avoidance of professional help.  Functional Impairment These difficulties may disrupt: academic/work performance,  romantic and social relationships,  self-esteem,  emotional regulation,  daily decision-making,  legal and ethical functioning.  Harm to Others When risk involves non-consenting individuals, minors, coercion, violence, or loss of control, the issue becomes not only clinical but also ethical and social. Core Message Hidden risk becomes more dangerous when help feels impossible. Existing Strengths Anonymous helplines  can reduce fear of disclosure and create a safer first contact.  Prevention programs  can reach help-seeking individuals before harm occurs. AI mental health tools may improve access, but  privacy, safety, bias, crisis response, and lack of human oversight  remain major concerns.  High-risk cases require more than automated support; they require  clear ethical boundaries and professional intervention . The system remains confidential as long as there is no serious, identifiable, or imminent risk. Process Flow 1. Anonymous Entry The user enters the system without giving unnecessary identifying information. Purpose: To reduce shame, stigma, and fear of disclosure. 2. Digital Informed Consent Before entering the module, the user signs a short digital consent form. The form explains: the system is not therapy or diagnosis, participation is voluntary, sensitive data is not stored without consent, confidentiality has limits in serious or imminent harm, high-risk cases may require a mandatory professional safety session, if imminent risk continues and the user refuses contact or acts toward harm, legal/safety authorities may be contacted. Purpose: To make the safety protocol transparent before the user begins. 3. Chatbot + Test + Game Module The user completes: chatbot-based exploration, literature-based screening test, game-like scenario tasks. Purpose: To identify hidden risk patterns and shift the user from impulse-driven reactions to reflective decision-making. 4. AI Pattern Summary The AI produces a short, non-diagnostic risk summary. Purpose: To guide the user toward the correct support pathway without labeling or diagnosing. 5. Risk-Level Decision The system classifies the case as: low risk, moderate risk, high risk, red risk. Purpose: To decide the appropriate level of support. 6. Mandatory Professional Safety Session For  high-risk and red-risk cases , the system first directs the user to a confidential professional safety session with a psychologist/psychiatrist. This session is still confidential, but focused on: immediate risk reduction, safety planning, impulse control, distance from potential victims, crisis support, referral to treatment. Purpose: To give the user a final clinical opportunity to choose safety before legal escalation. 7. Limited Safety Escalation If the user: refuses the mandatory safety session, cannot be reached after repeated contact attempts, reports imminent harm, moves toward an actual harmful act, or a specific identifiable person is at serious risk, then confidentiality becomes limited. At this stage, the system may alert: an on-call mental health professional, emergency services, child/adult protection services, or law enforcement, depending on the legal framework. Purpose: To prevent imminent harm while disclosing only the minimum necessary information. 8. Rehabilitation Credit / Legal Consideration For justice-involved users, participation in SafeBridge AI may be documented as a  rehabilitation-oriented risk reduction effort . This does  not automatically guarantee sentence reduction . However, it may be considered by legal authorities as evidence of: help-seeking, treatment participation, risk awareness, cooperation with safety planning, willingness for rehabilitation. Purpose: To encourage reintegration into society rather than only punishment. Bu kısmı böyle yazmamız çok önemli. Slide 10 — Risk Triage System Four-Level Risk Triage SafeBridge AI uses a four-level risk classification system. 1. Low Risk Thoughts, fantasies, or shame are present, but there is: no target person, no plan, no intent, no immediate danger. Response: Psychoeducation, shame reduction, trigger awareness, self-monitoring. 2. Moderate Risk Urges are strong, but the user does not want to harm anyone. Response: Impulse-delay tools, safety planning, avoiding triggers, same-day professional support suggestion. 3. High Risk There is a target person, opportunity, physical proximity, or fear of losing control. Response: Immediate safety steps, mandatory confidential professional safety session, crisis plan. 4. Red Risk Risk involves minors, non-consent, coercion, violence, actual harm, or imminent danger. Response: No further exploration, urgent human escalation, confidentiality limits activated. Key Message As risk increases, SafeBridge AI moves from supportive guidance to active safety intervention. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Bu modelin klinik omurgası risk sınıflaması. Düşük riskte kişi düşüncelerinden utanıyor olabilir ama davranışa geçme planı yoktur. Orta riskte dürtü güçlüdür ama kişi zarar vermek istemez. Yüksek riskte belirli kişi, fırsat veya kontrol kaybı vardır. Kırmızı riskte çocuk, rıza dışılık, zorlama, şiddet veya yakın tehlike söz konusudur. ( Internet Watch Foundation ) Slide 14 — Example User Pathways Case Scenario Applications Case 1 — Low Risk User A 20-year-old user feels shame about recurring thoughts but reports no target person, no plan, and no intention to act. SafeBridge AI Response: validates help-seeking, explains thought–urge–behavior distinction, opens psychoeducation module, suggests self-monitoring and optional professional support. Case 2 — Moderate Risk User A 23-year-old user reports strong urges and fear of losing control, but says they do not want to harm anyone. SafeBridge AI Response: opens impulse-control module, creates same-day safety plan, recommends professional support, encourages not being alone and avoiding triggers. Case 3 — High Risk User A user reports a specific risky situation, opportunity, or proximity to someone who may be harmed. SafeBridge AI Response: stops exploratory questions, gives immediate safety steps, requires confidential professional safety session, activates crisis planning. Case 4 — Red Risk User A user reports imminent harm, coercion, non-consent, minor involvement, or actual harmful action. SafeBridge AI Response: does not ask for details, does not continue game/test, activates urgent human escalation, confidentiality limits are applied. Key Message Different users do not need the same answer; they need the right level of safety. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Bu slayt sistemin çalıştığını gösteren örnek vaka slaytı. Düşük riskte kişiyi utandırmadan psikoeğitim veriyoruz. Orta riskte dürtü yönetimi ve aynı gün destek öneriyoruz. Yüksek riskte artık sohbeti kesip güvenlik planına geçiyoruz. Kırmızı riskte ise detay istemeden insan uzmana ve gerekli güvenlik mekanizmasına yönlendiriyoruz.” Burada vaka örneklerini kısa tut. Fazla detay verirsek hem etik olarak gereksiz olur hem de slaytın odağı kaçar. Slide 15 — Evaluation Plan How We Would Evaluate SafeBridge AI SafeBridge AI can be evaluated through a pilot study with ethical approval and professional supervision. Main Outcomes increase in help-seeking intention, increase in risk awareness, reduction in shame-based avoidance, improvement in consent and boundary understanding, increased willingness to contact professional support, successful completion of safety planning, appropriate escalation in high-risk cases. Process Measures module completion rate, chatbot engagement, number of professional referrals, number of completed safety sessions, user satisfaction, perceived safety and trust, false positive / false negative risk classifications. Safety Measures human review of high-risk cases, audit of escalation decisions, privacy and data protection review, adverse event monitoring. Key Message The model must be evaluated not only by engagement, but by safety, ethics, and real help-seeking outcomes. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Bu sistem gerçekten uygulanacaksa sadece kaç kişi kullandı diye bakamayız. Klinik, etik ve güvenlik çıktıları da ölçülmeli. Yardım arama niyeti arttı mı? Kişi riskini daha iyi fark etti mi? Utanç nedeniyle kaçınma azaldı mı? Yüksek risk vakaları doğru şekilde uzmana aktarıldı mı? Sistem hatalı sınıflandırma yaptı mı? Bunlar pilot çalışmada değerlendirilmeli.” Slide 16 — Ethical Safeguards and Data Protection Ethical Safeguards SafeBridge AI follows a safety-first and privacy-by-design approach. Data Protection anonymous entry by default, minimal data collection, no sensitive data storage without consent, encrypted data handling, user-controlled deletion when possible, separate handling of research data and clinical safety data. Clinical Safety no diagnosis by AI, no therapy claim, no harmful-detail collection, no behavior facilitation, mandatory professional review for high-risk cases, red-risk escalation protocol. User Rights informed consent, right to stop participation, transparent privacy limits, explanation of AI-generated summaries, access to human support. Key Message The system protects privacy, but not at the cost of safety. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Bu slaytta etik güvenlikleri netleştiriyoruz. Sistem minimum veri toplar, anonim girişle başlar, izinsiz hassas veri saklamaz. AI tanı koymaz, terapi yaptığını iddia etmez, zarar verici detay istemez. Kullanıcı baştan onam verir ve gizliliğin hangi durumlarda sınırlanabileceğini bilir. Bu modelde gizlilik çok önemlidir ama birinin güvenliği söz konusuysa gizlilik mutlak değildir.” WHO’nun sağlıkta AI rehberliği, geçerli bilgilendirilmiş onam, gizlilik ve insan kontrolünün korunmasını özellikle vurgular; bu yüzden SafeBridge AI’ın “privacy with limits” yapısı etik açıdan savunulabilir bir çerçeve sunar. ( Dünya Sağlık Örgütü ) Slide 17 — Limitations Limitations SafeBridge AI has important limitations. AI may misclassify risk. Users may provide incomplete or false information. Some users may avoid escalation or professional contact. Privacy and safety may conflict in high-risk situations. Legal reporting rules differ across countries. The model cannot replace clinical evaluation. Gamified modules may increase engagement, but must be carefully tested for validity and safety. Human supervision is required for high-risk decisions. Key Message SafeBridge AI is not a replacement for professionals; it is a structured bridge to them. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Bu sistemi mükemmel bir çözüm gibi sunmuyoruz. AI hata yapabilir. Kullanıcı doğru bilgi vermeyebilir. Bazı kişiler uzmana gitmek istemeyebilir. Hukuki bildirim kuralları ülkeden ülkeye değişebilir. Oyunlaştırılmış modülün de geçerliliği test edilmelidir. Bu yüzden SafeBridge AI klinik karar yerine geçmez; profesyonel yardıma köprü kurar.” Slide 18 — Conclusion Conclusion SafeBridge AI is an anonymous, AI-assisted early intervention and risk reduction model designed for individuals experiencing paraphilic tendencies or sexual impulse control difficulties. The model aims to: reduce shame-based silence, increase self-awareness, detect hidden risk patterns, support impulse control, prevent harm before it occurs, connect users to professional help, protect both individual dignity and public safety. Final Message SafeBridge AI does not label individuals. It identifies risk, creates a pause, and builds a bridge to professional help before harm happens. Türkçe Konuşma Notu “Sonuç olarak SafeBridge AI’ın amacı kişiyi etiketlemek değil, riski erken fark etmek. Sistem kişinin utanç nedeniyle saklanmasını azaltır, farkındalığını artırır, dürtüyle otomatik davranma yerine düşünme ve güvenli seçim yapma moduna geçmesini destekler. Risk yükseldiğinde ise insan uzmana ve gerekli güvenlik protokolüne yönlendirir.
